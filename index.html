Isabelle Ng

Computer Science — Boston University

Isabelle is a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at Boston University, advised by Professor Eshed Ohn-Bar. Before BU, she graduated from the University of California, Berkeley, where she received Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science, Data Science, and Music. Her main research interests include LLMs, multimodal models, and computer vision.

Besides research, she enjoys running and is training for 2 half marathons coming up. She also enjoys swimming, traveling, creating music, and singing in choirs.
